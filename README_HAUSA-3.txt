PHARMACONNECT NIGERIA — WEBSITE + ANDROID INSTALLABLE WEB APP (PWA)
Founder name shown in the starter: Yakubu Aliyu Adam

ABIN DA KE CIKIN WANNAN FAYIL
- index.html: shafin website
- styles.css: zane da yadda yake a waya/kwamfuta
- app.js: courses, search, notes, and quiz
- manifest.json + icon.svg: bayanan installable app
- sw.js: offline caching after first successful load on a supported browser

MUHIMMIN BAYANI
Wannan cikakken STARTER WEBSITE ne, ba cikakken native Android APK ba tukuna. Ana iya wallafa shi a internet, sannan a wasu browsers/wayoyi ana iya ƙara shi zuwa Home Screen a matsayin app (PWA). Ba a haɗa user accounts, cloud database, admin dashboard, payment, ads, analytics, ko Google Play publishing ba. Notes da quiz na farko ne kawai; a ƙara su tare da duba inganci kafin amfani da su a matsayin cikakken manhajar karatu.

YADDA ZA KA GWADA A WAYAR ANDROID
1. Sauke ZIP ɗin.
2. Buɗe Files / My Files > Downloads.
3. Danna ZIP ɗin ka zaɓi Extract/Unzip.
4. Buɗe folder ɗin da aka fitar. Idan wayarka ta ba da zaɓin buɗe index.html da Chrome, gwada shi. Wasu fasalolin PWA/offline ba za su yi aiki daga local file ba; sai an host website a HTTPS.

YADDA ZA A SAKA A INTERNET (MISALI GITHUB PAGES)
1. Ƙirƙiri/ shiga asusun GitHub.
2. Ƙirƙiri sabon public repository mai suna pharmaconnect-nigeria.
3. Upload dukkan files ɗin nan zuwa tushen repository (root), ba ZIP ɗin kaɗai ba.
4. A Settings > Pages, zaɓi deploy from branch, main branch, /(root), sannan Save.
5. Jira GitHub ya nuna URL ɗin website. Gwada shi a Chrome.
Menu da sunayen zaɓuɓɓuka na iya canzawa; ana iya amfani da Netlify maimakon GitHub Pages.

YADDA ZA A ƘARA SHI A HOME SCREEN
Bayan an wallafa shi ta HTTPS, buɗe website ɗin a Chrome Android. Danna menu ⋮ sannan ka nemi Install app ko Add to Home screen (sunan ya danganta da browser/waya).

KAFIN A SAKA ADS KO GOOGLE PLAY
- A buƙaci developer ya duba tsaro, accessibility, copyright, da privacy.
- Idan za a tara bayanan users, sai a tsara privacy policy da backend mai tsaro.
- Ads na bukatar asusun mai wallafa da bin dokokin network ɗin ads; ba a haɗa su a starter ba.
- Google Play native app yawanci yana bukatar Android package/build, signing, store listing, testing, da bin dokokin Play. Wannan ZIP ba APK ba ne.
- Kada a yi amfani da app wajen ba majiyyaci takamaiman shawarar magani. Tabbatar da notes ta hanyar qualified pharmacists/teachers da sahihan textbooks/guidelines.

MATAKIN GABA
Domin cikakken tsarin kasuwanci, ana bukatar a ƙara admin panel don saka sabbin notes/quizzes, database/cloud backend, login idan ya zama dole, privacy/security, sannan a gina Android package idan ana son APK na asali.
