OUR MISSION

Knowledge that supports better care.

PharmaConnect Nigeria is an educational project initiated by Yakubu Aliyu Adam to help Pharmacy and Pharmaceutical Technician students find approachable study resources in one place.

We aim to grow this platform with reviewed notes, more quizzes, and learning resources aligned with students’ curricula.

Important: This is an educational starter website, not a substitute for accredited teaching, official clinical guidance, a pharmacist, or a prescriber. Sample notes are introductory and must be checked against trusted textbooks and current local guidance.
